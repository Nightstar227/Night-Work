Night Work

Night work for a crew that never meets. Real-time, first-person heists where every vault door carries one lock per specialist. Take a contract, do your part from the shadows, and post it back for the next one.

Take a contractPick a role the job still needs. Each class can only clear its own lock and objectives.
Work in the darkGuards patrol, watch and listen. Light gives you away; noise draws them in. The Thief hides, the Warrior breaks, the Wizard sees.
Repost itGet back to the stairs and leave. Bodies, snuffed torches, sprung traps and broken bars stay as you left them.
PaydayWhen every lock is open, anyone can carry out the strongbox.

          Controls

          WASD move, mouse looks. Shift run (loud), C or Ctrl crouch. Left click strikes. Hold right click or Q to block; raise it just before a hit to parry. Space is your class move (Thief rolls, Warrior charges, Wizard blinks). R switches arrows. Swings, blocks, dodges and running cost stamina. E uses what you aim at. 1-4 abilities, 5-8 belt items. Esc pauses. Arrow keys also turn.
